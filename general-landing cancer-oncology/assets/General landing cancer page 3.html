Frequently asked questions

Questions patients often ask

Can I be completely cured of cancer?

Some cancers can be cured, and others may be controlled for long periods. The outlook depends on the type, stage, biology and response to treatment. A qualified oncologist can discuss realistic goals after reviewing your case.

Can I get a second opinion without travelling to India?

Often, yes. Depending on the hospital and specialist, records may be reviewed remotely and an online consultation may be available. The team will explain the process and any fees in advance.

What reports should I prepare?

Helpful documents may include a biopsy or pathology report, CT/MRI/PET imaging reports, molecular test results if available, recent blood tests and a summary of treatment already received. Do not delay contacting us if some reports are missing.

Will I receive a treatment estimate before travelling?

Hosplan can request an indicative estimate based on the available medical records. Final costs can change after in-person evaluation, additional tests or changes in the treatment plan.

Does Hosplan provide medical treatment?

No. Medical care is delivered by licensed doctors and hospitals. Hosplan helps coordinate specialist access, communication and patient travel services.

Can Hosplan help after I return home?

Our team can assist with coordinating follow-up communication and remote appointments with the treating hospital where available. Ongoing local medical care should be arranged with your own clinicians.
