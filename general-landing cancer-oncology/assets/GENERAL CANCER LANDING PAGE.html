The six questions almost every patient asks first

If your head is full of them right now, that is completely normal. Here is where we can help with each one, honestly.

Can I become cancer-free?

Many cancers can be cured, especially when treated at the right stage, and others can be controlled for years. The honest answer depends on your cancer type, stage, test results and general health. A specialist who has reviewed your actual reports can give you a realistic picture, not a general one.

See how a specialist opinion works

What is the best treatment for me?

Cancer is many different diseases, so the best plan is specific to you. It usually starts with confirming the diagnosis, modern molecular testing where relevant, and a review by surgeons, medical oncologists and radiation oncologists together. We help you reach that stage of the process.

How treatment plans are built

Whom can I trust?

Trust should be checkable. You can see doctors' names, qualifications and hospitals, check each hospital's accreditation with the accrediting body itself, and ask us how Hosplan is paid. You can also compare anything you are told with independent guides from ASCO, NCCN and the US National Cancer Institute.

How you can verify us

Who will take care of me?

Clinical care comes from your doctors. Everything around it, such as appointments, paperwork, travel, airport pick-up, hospital navigation and keeping your family informed, is handled by a named coordinator who stays in touch with you from the first message to your follow-up.

What our patient team does

How much will it cost, and how will I manage it?

No one can quote a responsible price without your reports. What we can do is request an itemised estimate from the hospital, show what is and isn't included, and tell you what could change it, so you can plan before you travel and avoid surprises.

Where do I get guidance as treatment goes on?

Many hospitals offer follow-up teleconsultations once you are home, and our team helps arrange them. For independent, trusted explanations at any time, ASCO's Cancer.Net and NCCN's patient guides are written by leading oncology bodies and are free to read.

Modern cancer care, chosen for you, not for the technology

New technology helps only when it fits your cancer. Your treating team decides what is medically right for you. These are the tools that may be considered.

Molecular and biomarker testing

Tests that look for changes in your tumour that can guide which medicines are most likely to help.

Targeted therapy

Medicines aimed at specific features of certain cancers.

Immunotherapy

Treatments that help the immune system respond to selected cancers.

Advanced radiation

IMRT, IGRT, SBRT and other precision techniques that aim radiation at the tumour while sparing healthy tissue, when indicated and available.

Minimally invasive and robotic surgery

Smaller incisions and, for suitable patients, quicker recovery.

Supportive and palliative care

Managing pain, nutrition and side effects so you can get through treatment more comfortably.

Questions worth asking any oncologist

Take these to any consultation, including ours. A good doctor will welcome them.

What exactly is my cancer type and stage, and how was it confirmed?

Ask to see the pathology report and what imaging was used. A second pathology review can matter.

Has my tumour been tested for molecular changes?

For some cancers, these tests can open up targeted or immunotherapy options. Ask whether they apply to you.

Is the goal of treatment cure, control or comfort?

Understanding the goal helps you weigh benefits against side effects.

What are all my options, including doing less or waiting?

Ask how the options compare on benefit, risk and recovery time.

Has my case been reviewed by a multidisciplinary tumor board?

For many cancers, combined review by several specialists leads to better-considered plans.

How much experience does this team have with my type of cancer?

It is reasonable to ask how many similar patients they treat.

What are the likely side effects and how will they be managed?

Ask who to contact if side effects start after you return home.

What is the total expected cost, and what could change it?

Ask for it in writing and itemised.

Frequently asked questions

Can I be completely cured of cancer?

Some cancers can be cured and others controlled for long periods. The outlook depends on the type, stage, biology and response to treatment. An oncologist can discuss realistic goals after reviewing your case.

Can I get an opinion without travelling to India?

Often, yes. Depending on the hospital and specialist, your records can be reviewed remotely and an online consultation may be available. We explain the process and any fees in advance.

Which reports should I prepare?

Helpful documents include a biopsy or pathology report, CT, MRI or PET reports, molecular test results if available, recent blood tests and a summary of any treatment already received. Please contact us even if some are missing.

Are the consultations free?

Some hospitals offer a complimentary initial case opinion. Scheduled video consultations may be chargeable. Availability and fees are confirmed before you book.

Will I get a cost estimate before I travel?

We request an indicative estimate from the hospital based on your medical records. Final costs can change after in-person evaluation, extra tests or changes to the plan.

Are your partner hospitals accredited?

Accreditation such as JCI or NABH applies to individual hospital facilities. We tell you which facility holds which accreditation, and you can verify it with the accrediting body.

Does Hosplan provide medical treatment?

No. Medical care is provided by licensed doctors and hospitals. Hosplan coordinates specialist access, communication and patient travel services.

Can Hosplan help after I return home?

We can help coordinate follow-up communication and remote appointments with the treating hospital where available. Ongoing local care should be arranged with your own clinicians.
